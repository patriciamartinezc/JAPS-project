How we make our clothes

JAPS runs on small batches. We produce twice a year, we never discount, and we publish the cost breakdown of every garment. If a piece does not sell, we remake it in a different colour instead of destroying it.

  1. We design and pattern the garment in Madrid.
  2. We order fabric from mills in Spain, Portugal and Italy.
  3. A family-run workshop near Porto sews the batch.
  4. We ship directly to you, with no middle warehouse.

Repairs are free for life. Bring a worn piece into any of our three stores and we will mend it while you wait.

Not sure about your size?

Our shirts run one size large on purpose. If you are between two sizes, take the smaller one. You can also email fit@japs.example with your measurements and we will answer within a day.
