Shirts and overshirts

Our shirts are cut wide through the body and short in the hem so they sit well untucked. Every one of them is made from a fabric we can name and trace. The autumn photographs show how they layer together.

The Linen Overshirt

Washed European linen, corozo buttons, boxy fit. 89 EUR. Available in sand, off-white and deep navy.

The Oxford Shirt

Heavy oxford cotton that softens with every wash, with a collar stiff enough to wear open. 75 EUR. Our best selling piece three seasons in a row.

Knitwear

Two jumpers, both spun from Spanish merino wool in Catalonia. We keep the range small so we can reorder the same yarn year after year instead of chasing whatever is cheapest.

The Merino Crewneck

Mid-weight merino, ribbed cuffs, no itch. 110 EUR. Machine washable on a cold wool cycle.

Care instructions

  • Wash cold, inside out, on a wool cycle
  • Dry flat, never on a radiator
  • De-pill with a comb after the first month
  • Free repairs for life in any JAPS store
