Where to find us

Every JAPS store carries the full collection in every size, so you can try a piece before ordering it. Walk in with a garment that needs mending and our tailor will fix it while you wait, free of charge, for as long as you own it.

Madrid - Calle del Pez 14

Our first shop and our workshop, in Malasana. The pattern room is behind the fitting rooms and you are welcome to look in.

Opening hours

  • Monday to Friday: 10:00 - 20:30
  • Saturday: 11:00 - 21:00
  • Sunday: closed

Barcelona - Carrer de Verdi 8

A smaller shop in Gracia, opened in 2024. It shares the building with a bookshop, so the entrance is the door on the left.

Opening hours

  • Tuesday to Saturday: 11:00 - 20:00
  • Sunday and Monday: closed

Porto - Rua de Cedofeita 220

Ten minutes from the workshop that sews our clothes. Alterations here are usually done the same morning.

Opening hours

  • Monday to Saturday: 10:00 - 19:00
  • Sunday: closed

Contact us

The fastest way to reach us is email; we answer every message within one working day. For questions about sizing, tell us your usual size in another brand and we will convert it.

  • General questions: hello@japs.example
  • Sizing and fit: fit@japs.example
  • Repairs and returns: repairs@japs.example
  • Phone, Madrid store: +34 910 000 000

We do not use social media for customer service, but we do post every restock on our Instagram account. Already a customer? Sign in to see your order history and repair tickets.

Returns

Thirty days, worn or unworn, no questions. Returns are free in Spain and Portugal, and cost 6 EUR everywhere else in Europe.
