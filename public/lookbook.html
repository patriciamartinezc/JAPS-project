Autumn 2026 and Spring 2026

The autumn photographs were taken on the roof of our workshop in Madrid in September 2026, in one afternoon, with no studio lighting. Everyone in these pictures works at JAPS or sews for us. You can buy every piece shown here on the collections page. The spring set was shot the previous season in Cádiz.

Behind the shoot

All our photographs are taken on film by the same photographer, and we publish them unretouched. We think clothes should look worn, not rendered.
