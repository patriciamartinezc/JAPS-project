What an account gives you

A JAPS account is mostly a record of what you already own, so our tailors know which fabric a piece was cut from when you bring it in to be mended.

  • Your order history and invoices
  • Your saved sizes, per garment
  • Restock emails for pieces you marked as sold out
  • The status of any repair you left in a store

We store nothing else. You can delete your account from this page at any time and we keep no copy of your data afterwards. We follow the guidance published by the Spanish Data Protection Agency.
